## Late-fusion behavior

The default now trains stacking weights from one OOF prediction per training
sample. STABL selection and downstream fitting occur wholly inside each fold;
groups remain assessment-disjoint. Full-training selectors and downstream
models are refitted only after weights are fixed. Expected fitting cost for
five-fold OOF plus the full refit is approximately six times the historical
single-fit path. On the fixed benchmark workload (60 samples, two 10-feature
omics, 10 bootstraps, three repetitions), median elapsed time was 0.378 s for
OOF (IQR 0.377--0.448) and 0.057 s for legacy (IQR 0.057--0.174), a 6.63x
ratio. Base-R GC maximum memory was about 198 MB versus 147 MB. The repetition
count is intentionally small and does not support a general speed claim.
`Rprof`, `Rprofmem`, call-stack summaries, allocation evidence, and the exact
manifest are stored under
`/exports/para-lipg-hpc/mdmanurung/stablr-validation-artifacts/0.1.1/performance`.
`late_fusion_training = "python_legacy"` preserves the historical in-sample
weighting and RNG behavior.

The first required 50-replicate paired OOF-versus-legacy matrix, Slurm job
`25315916`, failed its predeclared completeness gate: five of six cells
completed, while multinomial signal completed only 47/50 replicates because
rare classes made three selector fits ill posed. Its evidence is retained under
`/exports/para-lipg-hpc/mdmanurung/stablr-validation-artifacts/0.1.1/late-fusion/results`
and is not positive release evidence.

Before any rerun, the feasibility protocol was amended and recorded here:
classification simulations require at least ten observations per class in
both training and independent-test partitions; stacking folds and bootstrap
samples are outcome-stratified; per-omic selector failures inside a stacking
fold use that fold's training class prior or mean with explicit provenance;
and optimism is the directional train-minus-test difference rather than its
absolute value. These changes correct an ill-posed simulation and implement
the predeclared fallback contract; they do not change the noninferiority,
fallback-rate, or optimism gates and were not chosen to make an observed gate
pass. Independent test samples never enter selectors, downstream learners, or
stacking weights.

The replacement matrix, Slurm job `25316352`, ran from clean candidate
`6a5adac796c32c6c39575254d36086c80f1a63cb`. All six family/regime cells
completed 50/50 replicates. The fallback rate was zero in every cell; OOF
training reduced directional optimism and met the independent-test
noninferiority margin in every cell. Mean OOF-minus-legacy independent-test
differences were -0.00198 (binomial null), 0.00824 (Gaussian null), 0.00017
(multinomial null), 0.00534 (binomial signal), -0.00022 (Gaussian signal), and
0.01517 (multinomial signal). The manifest records a clean tracked tree and
SHA-256 hashes that were independently recomputed successfully. Raw artifacts
are stored under
`/exports/para-lipg-hpc/mdmanurung/stablr-validation-artifacts/0.1.1/late-fusion/candidate-6a5adac`.
